Our apps are designed to minimize data collection. Any personally identifiable information you provide is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Non‑Personal Information

When you use our apps, we may automatically collect:

This information helps us improve our services and identify potential issues.

Log Data

In case of app errors, we may collect diagnostic information including:

  • Device IP address

  • Time and date of the error
  • Other technical statistics
